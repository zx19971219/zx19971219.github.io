About Me

Hi! I’m Xuan Zhang (张璇), a Ph.D. candidate in Control Science and Engineering at the Department of Automation, Tsinghua University. I expect to graduate in June 2027.

My research interests include graph representation learning, generative recommendation, and relational intelligence. My recent work explores knowledge distillation, diffusion models, graph reasoning, and large language models for recommendation.

If you are interested in my research, please feel free to contact me at xuan-zha23@mails.tsinghua.edu.cn.
